支持 / Support

简体中文

如果你在使用「骐记待办」（英文名 Keeji）时需要帮助，或有功能建议，请发邮件联系开发者裴川锋：

peichuanfeng31@icloud.com

来信时请尽量说明设备型号、系统版本，以及遇到的问题。我们会在合理期限内回复。

订阅与退款

本应用仅提供自动续期订阅「骐记待办月度会员」（USD $2.99 / 月，以 App Store 显示的当地价格为准；新用户可免费试用 1 个月）。付款、续费和退款均由 Apple 处理。

  • 管理或取消订阅：打开 iPhone 或 iPad 的「设置」> Apple ID >「订阅」。
  • 如需退款，请通过 Apple 申请，并遵循 Apple 当时的退款政策。开发者无法在 Apple 流程之外独立完成退款。

数据与隐私

开发者不收集个人数据，也没有自有服务器。待办保存在你的设备上；若你开启了 iCloud 同步，数据由 Apple 在你自己的 iCloud 账户中处理。需要删除本机数据时，可在应用内删除或卸载应用；需要处理 iCloud 中的数据时，请使用系统的 iCloud 设置。

也可阅读：隐私政策 / Privacy、用户协议 / Terms。

English

If you need help with Keeji (Chinese name: 骐记待办), or have a feature suggestion, email the developer, Pei Chuanfeng (裴川锋):

peichuanfeng31@icloud.com

Please include your device model, system version, and a short description of the issue. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Subscriptions and refunds

The app offers a single auto-renewable subscription, Keeji Monthly Membership / 骐记待办月度会员 (USD $2.99 per month, or the localized price shown on the App Store, with a one-month free trial for new subscribers). Payments, renewals, and refunds are handled by Apple.

  • To manage or cancel a subscription, go to Settings on your iPhone or iPad > your Apple ID > Subscriptions.
  • To request a refund, please apply through Apple and follow Apple’s then-current refund policy. The developer cannot complete a refund independently of Apple’s process.

Data and privacy

The developer does not collect personal data and does not operate any servers. Tasks are stored on your device; if you enable iCloud sync, Apple processes the data in your own iCloud account. To remove local data, delete it in the app or delete the app. To manage iCloud data, use your system iCloud settings.

See also the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
